**Know exactly what to do next in Hypixel SkyBlock: the best gear you can use right now, your next upgrade, and everything you're working towards on a floating HUD.**

SkyBlock Progression remembers your stats (from your Hypixel API key, or just by opening menus once) and turns them into a clear plan for every skill, slayer, dungeon and money method.

## Features

- **Gear guide for every skill:** the best armor, weapon and tool you can use right now for Farming, Mining, Combat, Foraging, Fishing, Hunting and Dungeons, plus your next upgrade and what it needs.
- **Best area:** where to level each skill at your level.
- **Click an item to open its recipe** (uses SkyBlock's /viewrecipe).
- **Pin anything to a floating HUD:** pin a skill, slayer or item and see what you still need, how much XP is left and how many coins you're short. Drag it anywhere.
- **Next upgrades:** cheapest or most impactful upgrades first (gear, accessories, minions).
- **Accessories:** which ones you're missing that you can get now, and which to go for next, ranked by coins per magical power.
- **Minions:** exact coins per hour, time until full, chat alerts when they fill up, and time to afford your next tier.
- **Slayers, Dungeons, Kuudra and Trophy Fishing:** your levels, XP to next level and what the next level unlocks.
- **Fairy souls:** outlines and beams on the souls you haven't found yet, plus a list sorted by distance.
- **Money making:** methods you've unlocked, ranked with live bazaar prices.
- **Remembers your stats** and updates them when you level up.

Open the guide with **J** (rebindable) or **/sbprog**. Move the HUD with **/sbprog hud**.

## Is it allowed on Hypixel?

It only reads Hypixel's public API data and the menus you open, and shows it to you. It never clicks, buys or plays for you. Your API key is stored only on your computer. Hypixel doesn't officially approve any mods, so like every mod, use it at your own risk.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **26.1.x** with **Fabric Loader**. Works in Lunar Client's Fabric support, Prism, the CurseForge App and the vanilla launcher.
- Fabric API is **not** needed. The parts it uses are bundled inside the jar.
- Client-side only.
