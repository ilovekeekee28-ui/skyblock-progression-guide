## Reddit post (r/HypixelSkyblock)

**Title:** I made a free mod that tells you the best gear for your stats and tracks what you're working towards on a HUD

**Body:**

I kept alt-tabbing to wikis to figure out what to upgrade next, so I made a Fabric mod that does it in-game.

**What it does**
- For every skill (Farming, Mining, Combat, Foraging, Fishing, Hunting, Dungeons) it shows the best armor, weapon and tool you can use *right now*, the best area to level in, and your next upgrade with what it needs.
- **Pin anything to a floating HUD:** a skill, a slayer or an item. It shows what requirements you're still missing, XP to your next slayer level, and how many coins you're short.
- **Upgrades tab:** cheapest or most impactful upgrades first (gear, accessories, minions).
- **Accessories:** the ones you're missing that you can get now, ranked by coins per magical power.
- **Minions:** coins per hour, time until full, and a chat alert when they fill up.
- Slayer, Dungeons, Kuudra and Trophy Fishing progress, fairy soul outlines, and click any item to open its recipe.
- Remembers your stats. Add your API key, or just open your menus once.

**Is it allowed?** It only reads Hypixel's public API and the menus you open. It never clicks, buys or plays for you, and your API key stays on your computer. Like every mod, Hypixel doesn't officially approve it, so use it at your own risk.

**Requirements:** Minecraft 26.1.x with Fabric (works with Lunar Client). No Fabric API needed.
